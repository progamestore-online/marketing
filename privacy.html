Privacy Policy

We believe privacy is a right, not a feature.

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Overview

ProGameStore and all games hosted on the progamestore.online domain are designed with a strict no-data-collection policy. We do not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or behavioral data of any kind.

Data Collection

We collect no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • No usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No device fingerprinting
  • No IP address logging
  • No location data
  • No search history or browsing patterns

Cookies and Local Storage

Our games do not use cookies of any kind. Games may use your browser's local storage (localStorage or IndexedDB) solely to save your in-game progress and preferences on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with, embed, or load resources from any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no third-party scripts, pixels, beacons, or SDKs in our games.

Data Sharing

Since we collect no data, we have no data to share. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose any information to third parties because no such information exists in our systems.

Accounts

ProGameStore games use platform-level authentication (sign in with GitHub). Your GitHub username and avatar are used for identity. No additional personal data is collected or stored beyond what GitHub provides during OAuth.

Children's Privacy

Because we collect no behavioral data from any user, we inherently comply with all children's data protection requirements. Our games are safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, it will only become more protective of your privacy, never less. Our commitment to zero data collection is foundational to this platform.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through our GitHub organization.
